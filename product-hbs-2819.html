Product Overview

This console playstand is specially designed for Nintendo Switch 2, propping the console at a comfortable viewing angle for tabletop gaming, videos, and charging.

The stable ABS frame holds the console securely, while the open-back design supports airflow to help heat dissipate and leaves room for a charging cable during play. It raises the screen to a natural eye level for more comfortable tabletop use.

Lightweight and compact, the stand slips easily into a carrying case for travel and sets up instantly with no installation required, and its sturdy build keeps the console steady even when pressing controls.

A simple, universally useful accessory for home and on-the-go play.

Warm reminder: The Nintendo Switch 2 console and charging cable are not included.
