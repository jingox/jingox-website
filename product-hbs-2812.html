Product Overview

These ergonomic hand grips are specially designed for Nintendo Switch 2 Joy-Con controllers, sold as a pair to turn two small controllers into comfortable, full-size gamepads.

Slide a Joy-Con into each grip to enjoy a wider, more natural hold that improves control in action, racing, and platform games while reducing hand fatigue during long sessions. The enlarged handles fit adult hands comfortably, and the smooth ABS build feels solid and pleasant to hold.

Controllers insert and remove in seconds, and the two-pack is ready for local multiplayer right away. It is available in colorful or black finishes to match your style.

Lightweight, durable, and easy to carry — an essential comfort upgrade for every Switch 2 player.

Warm reminder: The Joy-Con controllers and Nintendo Switch 2 console are not included.
