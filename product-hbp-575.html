Product Overview

This RGB vertical stand is custom‑built for the PS5 Slim, compatible with both the disc edition and digital edition. It features stunning customizable RGB colorful lighting effects with three light modes.You can easily switch lighting modes via the built‑in touch control button to create a cool and immersive gaming atmosphere. The base is equipped with non‑slip silicone pads, which are wear‑resistant and greatly enhance stability, preventing the console from sliding or wobbling during use. Simple and hassle‑free installation requires no extra tools. Just align the bracket with the bottom buckle of your PS5 Slim, secure it with the included coin screw, connect the USB power cord and you are ready to go. With stylish outlook and reliable supporting performance, this vertical stand is an ideal decorative and practical accessory to organize and display your PS5 Slim console, perfectly upgrading your gaming setup.
