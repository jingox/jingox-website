Product Overview

Keep your ROG Xbox Ally X fully protected and neatly stored with this all-in-one carrying case accessory kit.

This rugged hard-shell travel bag features a tough molded EVA exterior. It delivers excellent shock absorption to defend against accidental drops, knocks and squeezing. The waterproof & stain-proof outer material resists daily spills and smudges; simply wipe stains with a damp cloth.

Custom molded interior securely holds your handheld console. Spacious mesh pocket stores charging cable, power adapter and other small accessories. Built-in carry handle makes it easy to take your gaming device anywhere for commute, trips or gaming gatherings.

Included protective accessories:
2pcs tempered glass screen protectors guard your screen from scratches; 4pcs non-slip thumb caps enhance gaming control; cleaning wipes and dust stickers help you install screen film cleanly without dust.
