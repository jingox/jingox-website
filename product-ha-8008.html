Product Overview

This integrated stand for PS5 and PS5 Slim consoles is a one-stop solution for PS5 players on their desktops.
The stand features a touch-sensitive adjustable cooling fan, a unique bottom air intake channel that accelerates air circulation and quickly lowers the console temperature; two controllers can be charged simultaneously, and the LED indicator lights clearly show the battery status. It comes with a controllable RGB ambiance light to create a cool gaming atmosphere.
The side headphone holder can store headphones and they can be accessed easily; multiple expansion interfaces enable data transmission and power supply for external devices. The anti-slip silicone pad on the bottom and the fixing screws firmly secure the console, preventing it from sliding or tipping over, and protecting the console from damage.
It can be quickly assembled without additional tools, integrating cooling, charging, lighting, and storage functions into one, easily creating a neat and cool gaming desktop.
