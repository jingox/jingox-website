About JINGOX

A Shenzhen source factory and trading partner for wholesale gaming console accessories.

JINGOX is the wholesale brand of Shenzhen Zhimi Technology Co., Ltd., based in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. We focus on accessories for the world's leading game consoles, combining factory production with trading so buyers can source both ready catalog items and custom products in one place.

Our product line spans charging docks, cooling stands, controller and console storage, somatosensory kits, faceplates, protective cases and storage bags for Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, Xbox and VR/Meta Quest platforms.

We work with distributors, retailers, online sellers and brand owners. The ready catalog is available with a low minimum order — even a single sample — while our OEM/ODM team supports custom color, logo, packaging, bundles and product development.

Every order is backed by material and function checks, consistent specifications and a direct sales contact that responds quickly. We're here to make sourcing gaming accessories simpler, faster and more reliable.
