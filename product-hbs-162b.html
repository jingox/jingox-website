Product Overview

The crystal clear protective case is made of premium highly transparent hard PC material. The crystal‑clear appearance displays the original look and color of your Switch Lite.
The front and back covers adopt snap‑on design, tightly attached to the console without wobbling. All ports and buttons are precisely reserved, so you can charge or plug in headphones conveniently without taking off the shell.
The tough PC material guards the device against scratches and daily collision damage and resists yellowing after long‑time use.
Slim and lightweight, it keeps the original comfortable hand‑feel and is convenient for storage and carrying when going out.
The smooth surface can be cleaned effortlessly.
Note: Only the clear PC protective case is included. Nintendo Switch Lite console is NOT included.
