Product Overview

This combo set is specially designed for Nintendo Switch 2 Joy-Con controllers, pairing two racing steering wheels with two ergonomic hand grips for versatile gameplay across genres.

Use the wheels for racing titles and swap in the hand grips for action, platform, and multiplayer games, enjoying improved comfort and control in every situation. The four-piece set is ready for two-player play right out of the box.

Made of durable black and colorful ABS with a solid build, every piece attaches in seconds and is built to last, and the coordinated set offers great variety and value in one purchase.

A complete control bundle and an excellent gift for Switch 2 players.

Warm reminder: The Joy-Con controllers and Nintendo Switch 2 console are not included.
