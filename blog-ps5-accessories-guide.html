PS5 Accessories: The Complete Buyer's Guide for PS5, PS5 Slim & PS5 Pro (2026)

PS5 accessories fall into four groups: cooling stands, controller charging docks, replacement faceplates, and storage holders, with 18 models

PS5 Accessories · November 12, 2026 · 3 min read · Category: PS5 Accessories

Quick answer

PS5 accessories fall into four groups: cooling stands, controller charging docks, replacement faceplates, and storage holders, with 18 models in the current range. The key decision when buying is console compatibility, because some pieces fit the original PS5 only while others are built for the PS5 Slim and PS5 Pro, often stated directly in the product name. After confirming the console model, choose by placement (vertical or wall-mounted), whether you want RGB lighting, and the material. This guide covers every type and how to choose the correct one for PS5, PS5 Slim, or PS5 Pro.

PS5, PS5 Slim, and PS5 Pro: the accessory difference

The three consoles differ in size and shape, so accessories are not universally interchangeable. Some cooling stands are designed for all three, such as HA-8008 and HBP-534, while others are built specifically for the PS5 Slim, such as HBP-537 and the wall-mounted HBP-545. The supported console is stated on each product and should be confirmed before ordering. The full comparison is on the difference between PS5, Slim, and Pro accessories.

How to choose PS5 accessories

Check these five points in order.

  1. Confirm the console model. Match the accessory to PS5, PS5 Slim, or PS5 Pro; never assume a Slim piece fits the original console.
  2. Choose the accessory function. Cooling stands for airflow and combined charging, charging docks for controllers, faceplates for appearance, storage stands for placement.
  3. Decide placement. Choose a vertical base or a wall-mounted bracket such as HBP-545 depending on available space.
  4. RGB or standard. Some models, including HBP-558 and HA-8008, include RGB lighting, while others are plain.
  5. Material and fit. Confirm the accessory fits the console firmly, especially for faceplates and wall brackets.

Frequently asked questions

Do PS5 accessories fit both Slim and Pro? Only models designed for all three consoles, such as HA-8008 and HBP-534, do so; Slim-specific pieces are not universal.

What is the difference between a cooling stand and a charging dock? A cooling stand focuses on airflow and often adds charging, while a charging dock only powers the controllers and related devices.

Can I change the PS5 shell myself? Yes, replacement faceplates are designed to be fitted by the user, with separate sets for the standard and Slim consoles.

Can I order a single sample first? Yes, there is no minimum order quantity, so one sample can be checked before a bulk order.
