Product Overview

Product introduction
A carrying case tailored for Nintendo Switch/Switch Lite, ideal for carrying game consoles when you go out.The body of the bag is made of wear-resistant polyester oxford cloth fabric, waterproof and scratch-resistant, strong and durable.
The back cushion is designed with three layers of composite materials: the outer layer is breathable mesh, the middle layer is cushioning sponge, and the inner layer is decompression and seismic sponge.Efficient ventilation and perspiration, relieve the pressure on the shoulders, keep the back from sweating for a long time, and make it more comfortable to carry.
Multi-compartment large-capacity storage design, you can put down the Switch console, and the front double pockets can store game cassettes, chargers, data cables, headphones and other accessories; the inner hidden mezzanine can also store mobile phones and other small portable objects, and the items are stored in partitions, which is simple and convenient to take.
Smooth metal zipper with ABS buckle, adjustable and widened shoulder strap, one-shoulder messenger, two carrying methods on the chest.Trendy camouflage printing appearance, fashionable and lightweight, suitable for commuting, traveling, and going out to play.
