Product Overview

This custom replacement faceplate brings a stunning new look to your game console. Specially designed for the PS5 Disc Edition,you can easily create a personalized gaming console and turn your gaming device into an eye‑catching decoration for your living room.

Constructed from ABS material, the shell features a solid, impact‑resistant structure. It effectively guards your console against bumps, scratches and daily abrasion, resisting deformation and cracks over long‑term use and offering reliable outer‑layer protection.

Precision one‑piece molding perfectly aligns with the original buckles of the game console. The original heat dissipation air ducts are fully reserved without blocking airflow, so the cooling performance of your PS5 will not be compromised. No extra tools are required. You can finish installation and removal by following simple steps, and the console body will not get scratched during replacement.
