How do Joy-Con sports accessories work

Joy-Con sports accessories are passive holders with no electronics

Somatosensory · November 12, 2026 · 3 min read · Category: Somatosensory

Quick answer

Joy-Con sports accessories are passive holders with no electronics. A Joy-Con slides into a shaped wheel, racket, grip, or strap, and the controller's own motion sensors read the player's movement. The accessory supplies the correct grip and angle for the sport, while the Joy-Con tracks the swing, steering, punch, or throw. The same two Joy-Con can be moved between different accessories for different games.

The Joy-Con supplies the sensing

Each accessory positions the Joy-Con at the angle that best represents the real-world object, such as a racket or steering wheel, so the motion data matches the intended action.

Move controllers between accessories

Because the pieces are passive, two Joy-Con can be shared across a wheel, racket, gun, or rod rather than needing a separate electronic device for each sport.
