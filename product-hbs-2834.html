Product Overview

These universal hand grips are compatible with Nintendo Switch, Switch OLED, and Switch 2 Joy-Con controllers, offering broad cross-generation compatibility in one pair.

Each grip enlarges a small Joy-Con into a comfortable, ergonomic handle for better control and less hand fatigue across action, racing, and multiplayer games. Controllers slide in and out quickly, and the left/right pair is ready for two-player play.

Made of smooth, lightweight black ABS at just 53.6g, the grips are durable and easy to carry in any case, and their universal fit means one accessory works across multiple console generations.

A versatile, dependable comfort upgrade and a smart inventory choice.

Warm reminder: The Joy-Con controllers and Nintendo Switch consoles are not included.
