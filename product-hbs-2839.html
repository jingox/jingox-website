Product Overview

These tennis rackets are specially designed for Nintendo Switch 2 Joy-Con controllers, sold as a pair with wrist straps for more immersive sports gameplay.

Each racket holds one Joy-Con so you can swing, serve, and volley with realistic motions in tennis and sports games. The large racket face improves motion control, and the included wrist straps secure the controllers during active play for safety.

Made of smooth, durable ABS in colorful or black finishes at only 88.4g for the set, the rackets are light in the hands and quick to attach.

A family-friendly sports essential that brings active, two-player fun to any home.

Warm reminder: The Joy-Con controllers and Nintendo Switch 2 console are not included.
