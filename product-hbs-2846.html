Product Overview

This multifunctional storage stand is specially designed for Nintendo Switch 2, organizing controllers, game cards, and accessories in one stable tower.

Dedicated compartments hold gear upright and sorted, so controllers and games are easy to find, grab, and put away, eliminating desktop clutter. The substantial ABS frame provides a stable, wobble-free base that sits securely on any desk or entertainment center.

Its vertical design saves space while displaying a gaming collection neatly, and the durable black build is made for long-term daily use.

A versatile organization hub that pairs naturally with any console and accessory purchase.

Warm reminder: The controllers, game cards, and Nintendo Switch 2 console are not included.
